# bQuery Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want bQuery to be a welcoming, professional, and constructive project for everyone who participates. Contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to help create an environment where people can collaborate safely, ask questions, share ideas, and receive feedback without harassment or hostility.

We are committed to treating everyone with respect regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, neurotype, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual orientation, or other personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

When participating in this project, please:

- Be respectful, patient, and considerate in discussions and reviews.
- Assume good intent while still giving clear, honest, actionable feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Be open to different viewpoints, experience levels, and approaches.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologize when appropriate, and help resolve conflicts.
- Keep technical discussions on topic and communicate in English when contributing to repository content.
- Respect the time and boundaries of maintainers and other contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable in project spaces or related one-to-one interactions:

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks.
- Insults, slurs, inflammatory language, or deliberately demeaning comments.
- Trolling, baiting, dogpiling, or repeated disruptive behavior.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Publishing or threatening to publish someone else's private or identifying information without explicit permission.
- Bad-faith reviews, deliberate misrepresentation, or repeated attempts to derail discussions.
- Spam, off-topic promotion, or abuse of issues, pull requests, discussions, or review channels.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern or participates in an investigation.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:

- GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and reviews
- Documentation, examples, and linked community spaces managed for the project
- Public and private communication that is directly connected to project collaboration

It also applies when an individual is representing the project in public spaces.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for setting the tone of project interactions and for enforcing this Code of Conduct fairly and consistently. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, pull requests, discussions, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers should:

- Explain moderation decisions when appropriate
- Protect reporters' privacy as much as possible
- Avoid public escalation when a private resolution is more effective
- Act in good faith and with proportionality

## Reporting concerns

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it to the maintainers at [support@josunlp.de](mailto:support@josunlp.de).

When possible, include:

- A description of what happened
- Links or screenshots if available
- The people involved
- Any relevant context about urgency or safety concerns

Reports will be reviewed as promptly as practical. We will make a good-faith effort to acknowledge reports, assess the situation, keep information limited to those who need it, and determine an appropriate response.

## Enforcement

Possible responses to violations may include:

1. A private reminder or request to stop the behavior
2. A warning and clarification of expected conduct
3. Removal of content, locking conversations, or limiting participation
4. Temporary suspension from project spaces
5. Permanent ban from project spaces or contributions

Enforcement decisions will consider context, severity, pattern of behavior, and impact on the community.

## Good-faith participation

Constructive disagreement is part of healthy open source collaboration. A report made in good faith will never, by itself, be treated as a violation. Likewise, contributors are expected to accept moderation outcomes professionally, even when they disagree with them.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is a project-specific policy for bQuery informed by common open source community standards.
